# iamisi – notes for working on this repo

(Formerly iamSubPilot, before that HADAL. The name: "isi" stands for Isabella and sounds like the English "easy" – light, carefree; this is explained in the info text after the title screen (`infoP2`). Internal names such as the `hadal.*` localStorage keys and the PeerJS prefix `hadal-v1-` stay as they are, so saved progress and rooms keep working. Repo `Pilot-2026/iamisi` (renamed from iamSubPilot), Pages https://pilot-2026.github.io/iamisi/, Netlify site iamisi.netlify.app (renamed from iamsubpilot – `js/api.js` tries both names, `MP_SITES` in config.js; the functions' CORS accepts both).)

Browser game (three.js, plain ES modules, no build step), hosted on GitHub Pages from `main`.
Owner: Andreas Schmölzer (pilotFilm). Must stay completely separate from his other games (own repo, own Firebase project). Touch controls: up/down at the far left (left thumb), the action buttons next to them, the joystick on the right – kept compact so they cover little of the sea (sizes per screen in the block at the end of `style.css`: tablet 54 px buttons / 120 px joystick, phone sideways 46/104, phone upright 44/100 in two low rows, ≤380 px 40/92; the knob travel in `input.js` is 0.32 × the joystick width). The 3D canvas is sized by CSS and its drawing buffer follows `clientWidth/Height` every frame (`fitView`, `vw()/vh()` in main.js) – iPhone Safari doesn't always update `innerHeight`.

## Structure
- `index.html`, `style.css` – shell, HUD markup, styles (mobile + desktop; on extremely wide screens ≥ 2.6:1 the whole HUD sits in a centred 2.6 × height area – `--edge`, `#hud` with transform); menus and dialogs are yellow glass (`--sun-glass`), wordmark `.wm` ("iam" #ffff00 with black outline, "isi" #000088 50 % bigger, roundel `assets/roundel.svg` as big as "isi"); the title screen dedication and the "made with AI" line sit together in a transparent yellow box (`.intro-dedic`)
- `privacy.html` – privacy policy (DE + EN); imprint is https://www.pilotfilm.at/impressum
- `assets/` – roundel, logos and portrait for the footer bar (identical to iamPilot's), `fonts/` – Source Sans 3 (self-hosted, no Google Fonts)
- Team data is state, not just events: player fields `md` (finished missions, `missionKey`), `ph` (own photos), `col` (own finds `[item id, kind]`), `atl`; all scoped to one dive by `dv` (= the dive's seed, reset at every dive start) – teammates and latecomers take everything over, nothing from an earlier dive in the same room counts
- `js/main.js` – menu (footer bar = `footerHtml()`, same links/logos/social buttons as iamPilot), profile, `Dive` class (game loop per dive, `collide()` + `drawRadar()`), results, quiz, multiplayer lobby; in a dive a lone tap (no second tap within 0.45 s, `tapToggle`) folds the mission list to its title line (count + chevron, the deepest-point guide stays; remembered as `hadal.mfold`) and a lone tap makes the radar big (`.big`; on upright phones it moves to the middle); quick series of taps never toggle, so the secret sequence always hits, and it restores both afterwards (`fold`, `seq.snap`). The action button "End" (`#bSurface`, key B) asks first (`confirmEnd`), the pause menu's surface/abort buttons don't
- `js/collide.js` – capsule shapes worked out from the 3D models, segment–segment tests (subs bounce off each other, animals/objects/ship hull are solid)
- `js/world.js` – trench terrain (heightfield; rugged walls: stepped ledges + ridges in `depthFn`, only on the walls – plain and flat bottom stay smooth), solid rock outcrops and ledges on the walls (`buildRocks`, instanced, `pushOut` ellipsoid collision called from `Dive.collide`), light/fog by depth, sonar mapping shader, marine snow
- `js/sub.js` – movement capped at 500 km/h horizontally even with boost, strong headlight (spot 150, range 70–110); eight original sub designs (`SUB_MODELS`: isabella (mermaid sub, no. 1, built from `js/merfolk.js`, laughing face, no claw: grabs with her right hand – `info.hand` → `updateHandGrab`, the find floats into her palm), hummel, wolfshark (2nd in the picker, name plates "WOLFSHARK"), puffer, narwhal, octopus, rocket, ufo; merged parts via `js/parts.js`), movement; `RemoteSub` for teammates (model sent as player field `sub`)
- `js/launch.js` – launch from the ship (HMS Andrew): A-frame, chains, splash, underwater transition (skippable); `Recovery` = the reverse when surfacing (crane lifts the sub back on deck, then results)
- `js/schools.js` – instanced fish schools (decorative, upper ocean + twilight glow shoals)
- `js/intro.js` – title screen / menu background aquarium (the wordmark "iamisi" assembles itself letter by letter: one plane per letter + roundel coin, "isi" and the coin `ISI` = 1.5 × bigger on a shared baseline, `TEXT_W` measured in `buildTitle`, `layout()` fits `titleH`/`titleMid` into the free band, water shader, animals, subs; `?ogshot=1` = crowded posed scene used to shoot the link preview `assets/og-image.jpg`, 1200×630, keep it under 300 KB for WhatsApp; og/twitter meta tags in `index.html`)
- `js/creatures.js` – fantasy creature models, spawning, behaviour, photo/grab targeting
- `js/models.js` + `js/detail.js` – detailed real animals (incl. 10 shark species from one `buildShark()` config) and seafloor objects (lofted, vertex-painted bodies, fins, eyes); keep the userData animation hooks (fluke, tail, wings, flips, fins, bell, eyes, ears, cape, glow, strobe)
- `js/merfolk.js` – mermaid figure (`buildMerFigure` + `animMer`: segmented tail, fluke, fins, arms with strokes and waving, hair chains, blinking) in two palettes (ISABELLA sub / mermaid of Atlantis) and Neptune (`buildNeptune` + `animNeptune`: beard, crown, cape, merman tail, glowing trident). Neptune and the Atlantis mermaid are secret: only in Atlantis, never on the title screen
- `js/atlantis.js` – Atlantis, modelled on the owner's two reference pictures: pale stone + gold, facades with lit Gothic windows (canvas textures), ribbed glass domes (shader), spiral tower, glowing crystal tower, glass tubes with gold rings + pods, aqueducts, mosaic plaza with columns/statues/fountain (Neptune), glowing corals, kelp (shader sway), light shafts, whales/mantas/dolphins/fish, vehicles with headlights, a bright water backdrop. One variant per trench (`THEMES`: colours, centrepiece grandDome/crystalTower/spiral/crystals/ringCitadel/twinTowers/domeCluster, building mix, name EN/DE shown in the toast); the dive seed shuffles the layout. Static parts are merged per material (`Merger`). Every centrepiece faces the plaza; its secret door is hidden at a side or the back in a gap of the architecture (`gapDoor`, never on the plaza side where Neptune looks), a plain dark stone doorway with only a faint shimmer (`M.portalHidden`, no lamps/gold; the exit inside the hall stays bright) (`makeDoor`, `portalCheck`): swimming in hides the city and shows the hidden hall (`buildHall`: starry dome, columns, treasure, mystic spotlight cones, three coloured lamps, way out through the same door; the hall grows with its creature, sub + camera are kept inside, `enter`/`exit`). On entering, the creature's legend shows first as a parchment card beside it (left on big screens) until tapped, then the positive sentences start in bubbles beside its face (`placeBubble(el, pos, side)`). The hall's dome is glass: through it you see bright water, a ring of Atlantis towers, light shafts and whales/mantas/dolphins circling over the hall (`buildHallOutside`). The hall has a light show (`buildHallFx`: turning fan of rays from the apex, sweeping beams, drifting orbs, floor caustics, 10 spotlights), 12 treasures to collect by swimming through them and one secret per trench that only a sonar ping reveals (`buildLoot`, `HALL_SECRETS`, `sonarPing`, events via `takeEvents` → counter `#hallCount`, score row "Atlantis treasures"); surfacing from inside leaves the hall automatically and ignores the domes (stuck → floats up anyway). In the hall lives the trench's fable creature (`js/fables.js`, in trench order: Moon-Pearl Dragon/Bakunawa, Captain Bushybeard (Kapitän Rauschebart, the friendly pirate from the owner's bedtime stories for Isabella: tricorn, eye patch, hook, wooden leg, a very big tricorn, a big skull-and-crossbones belt buckle, snow-white beard down to the chest made of hundreds of fine strands (`hairMesh`: tapered tubes in one mesh, sway in the vertex shader; moustache, brows and hair the same), a crew member on his shoulder; his hall also has 100 hopping yellow crew members "Kicherlinge" (two `InstancedMesh`es; swim through one and it jumps for joy with a double flip and pops into the sub – `onCatch`, counted) and the flying sea fairy Princess Perla (blonde, white gown with lace and pearls, golden crown, textured wings, star wand; twice the sub's length) via `makeExtras` – both original designs, not Minions or Lillifee; `source: []`), Ice Kraken, Captain Eight-Arms/colossal octopus, Umibōzu, Flying Dutchman ghost ship (`turn`), Leviathan – 4 × the sub's length, legend text + sources, `FABLE_SPEECH` = the owner's positive-thinking sentences in EN/DE, shown as yellow speech bubbles with the player's name; the creature must never be visible from outside). Appears when every mission of a dive is done (cheat: 5 × key 1, or on screen exactly 5 taps on the mission list then 5 on the radar; in Atlantis the same backwards – 5 × radar then 5 × missions, or 5 × key 1 – jumps to the fable creature); the radar is shown in every dive (deepest point ★, Atlantis A); the results show a picture of Atlantis (`snapAtlantis`) and the beings met there (Neptune, mermaid, fable creature, with the captain also a crew member and Perla as turntables); energy/hull/oxygen stay full there; `world.magic` clears the water near it (`world.magicCol` = theme water); buildings are solid (`cols`, `clampSub`). Team dives share the spot (player field `atl`)
- `js/santa.js` – Christmas special: `xmasSeason()` (17 Dec – 2 Jan, `?xmas=1` forces it), `buildSanta()` (sleigh with gold scrollwork, runners, lanterns, garland lights, sack with presents/teddy/candy cane, Santa with spectacles, beard, floppy hat, waving; four seahorses with coronet, ringed body, curling tail, fluttering fins, harness + bells, reins, snowflake trail), `decorateSub()` (Santa hat – on Isabella attached to her head –, garland with blinking lights, candy cane). In season Santa visits every dive (`Dive.updateSanta`: swims in, circles the sub, white speech bubble `#santaBubble` "Hohoho, fröhliche Weihnachten, <name>!", jingle) and the title aquarium. Cheat any time: 5 × key 2 or 10 taps on your own sub → Santa comes and only then is your own sub dressed up (player field `xm`, teammates see it)
- `js/guide.js` – a soft marker over the deepest point that only fades in within ~400 m (the guide under the missions and the radar star show the way; the flat deepest basin counts as reached) and the map overlay (key K / 🗺: soft fog over unexplored parts, route, finds, teammates, Atlantis, wrecks seen, team challenges – the deepest point is not on the map)
- `js/viewer.js` – separate small WebGL view: rotating 3D discovery card (a new photo shows a small card for 5 s while you keep diving – press and hold it to keep it; `peek` in `showCard`; in the logbook/results the big card), every tile on the results screen (photos and Atlantis beings) opens it big, sub picker hover spin, `turntable()` sprite strips for the rotating photo tiles on the results screen
- `js/stats.js` + `js/statsview.js` + `js/data/tzcountry.js` – anonymous visitor statistics (Firestore counters `stats/all`, `stats/d_YYYY-MM-DD`, collection `visits`; local copy as fallback; owner device marked on opening, counted separately). Opened with 5 × key 9 or 5 taps on the version number; one scrolling field with a section menu (overview, returning visitors from `/api/visitor` – each device has an anonymous random number `hadal.vid`, own devices marked 🏠 –, latest visits, countries, times, devices & languages, game). The Firestore `visits` rule only allows its six fields – don't add fields there without updating `firestore.rules` in the console
- `js/wrecks.js` – a broken WWII-style warship (two hull halves, turrets, bridge, funnel) and a crashed four-engine plane near the deep end of every trench (generic designs, no markings), big enough to swim into, 12 treasures inside collected by swimming through (score row "Wreck treasures", Dr. Echo line when found, map icons)
- `js/challenges.js` – 10 team challenges, co-op dives only (`Dive.team`): each has two ✋ handles; one sub at each handle for 1.6 s → done (net with dolphin, turtle in a ghost net, giant clam, treasure chest, boulder before a cave, whale in a rope, barrel, stuck lander, twin crystals, temple doors). Occupancy is worked out from everybody's sub positions on every client; `chdone` event + player field `chd` (finished ids, also for latecomers); radar/map icons, mission line, 300 points each
- `js/companions.js` – company in the other fable halls (`companionExtras` is the default `makeExtras` in `buildFable`): a swarm of 60 little creatures of the legend to collect (Drachlinge, Kräkchen, Oktopiraten, Mönchlein, Geisterlein, Leviathänchen – instanced, joy spin when caught) and a flyer about Perla's size with a meaning for the legend (moon, Havfrue, parrot Polly, lantern spirit, Captain Van der Decken, the Ziz); `FLYERS` info shown by Dr. Echo after the legend and on the results tiles (`comp:` keys in viewer.js)
- `js/cloud.js` – cloud profiles: the same diver name on any device loads the same research points, upgrades, sub, logbook and dives (`syncProfile` in main.js on start and after the name; merge = keep the best of both; switching to a name with cloud progress replaces local progress; pushes debounced after `saveProfile`; never age/language; off for `?autostart` and localhost unless `?cloud=1`). Server: `netlify/functions/profile.mjs` (GET ?name, POST, GET ?list=1 → "Divers" tab in the leaderboard) and `visitor.mjs` (returning-visitor list), data in Netlify Blobs (`@netlify/blobs`), helpers in `netlify/lib/profile.mjs`
- `js/scientist.js` + `js/data/knowledge.js` – Dr. Echo (local knowledge base, optional `AI_ENDPOINT`)
- `js/data/*.js` – levels, creatures (facts EN/DE + sources), quiz (age tiers kid/junior/adult; `quiz-more.js` = many more questions, each with `src` shown after answering, plus `FABLE_QUESTIONS` – one bonus question about the legend when the fable creature was met; 4 + 1 questions per debrief)
- `js/net.js` – rooms: **LiveKit** (default, like iamPilot but its own LiveKit project + own Netlify site: functions `netlify/functions/{join,rooms,meta}.mjs`, keys only in Netlify env vars `LIVEKIT_URL/KEY/SECRET`, setup in ONLINE-SETUP.md; every call to the Netlify functions (rooms and cloud) goes through `apiFetch` in `js/api.js`, `?mpapi=` for tests, `?p2p=1` forces the fallback; host keeps meta + clock, newcomers get `sync`, positions lossy, the rest reliable; drop-outs are tolerated: a participant who disconnects is kept 45 s (lobby host 15 s), our own lost connection is re-opened with the same token (`lkRejoin`, ~90 s), everybody re-sends the whole player record every 4 s (`lkKeepFresh`), while the host is away the longest-present diver answers newcomers (`lkResponder`), the host leaving mid-dive doesn't end the dive for the others; radio publishes one gated audio track), else Firebase RTDB if configured, else peer-to-peer (PeerJS, host relays, all rooms public: codes from `PUBLIC_CODES`). `listRooms()` sends one small probe per code through the signalling server (`CANDIDATE` with `payload.probe`); hosts answer the same way with their room summary, empty codes come back as `peer-unavailable` (~5 s); taken codes without an answer get a data-connection fallback and are otherwise listed as `unreachable`. `checkConnection()` = server/STUN/TURN check shown in the multiplayer window. ICE = Google STUN + PeerJS TURN (UDP + TCP). Lonely host lobbies merge (smaller code wins); screen wake lock while in a room. `window.__FAKE_PEER__` hook for tests; `?local=1` = BroadcastChannel between tabs; Firestore leaderboard or local
- `js/config.js` – `FIREBASE_CONFIG`, `AI_ENDPOINT` (never put secrets here – public repo)
- `js/radio.js` – push-to-talk voice (PeerJS media calls between the room's peers, own channel – unrelated to the glider game's LiveKit radio) + quick radio calls; voice only for age 14+
- `js/audio.js` – synthesised FX + generative music: `TRACKS` (12 cheerful major/mixolydian/lydian pieces, `whales`/`dolphins` = how often whale song and dolphin squeaks join in) rotate, each start/scene begins with the next one (`hadal.track`); whale song, dolphins, echo sounder always on top; sound modes all/fx/music/off (HUD button `#btnSound`, key M); ISABELLA has no engine hum, only a quiet water rush and a whomp-and-swish exactly when her fluke sweeps through the middle (`setFinMode`, `finSwish` when `rig.ph - 4.2` passes a multiple of π)
- `js/vendor/` – bundled three.js, Firebase and PeerJS (built with esbuild from npm)

## Rules
- Every real fact needs a source (`source` on creatures). Fantasy creatures must stay clearly marked (`kind: 'fantasy'`).
- All UI text in both languages (`js/i18n.js`, `{en, de}` objects in data files).
- Units: 1 world unit = 10 m. Depth in metres = `-y * 10`.
- Keep creature ids deterministic (seeded RNG) – multiplayer relies on identical spawns.
- Never copy protected designs or characters (e.g. the Beatles' Yellow Submarine): the sub is an original bumblebee design.

## Testing
LiveKit end to end: run a local `livekit-server --dev --bind 127.0.0.1 --node-ip 127.0.0.1` (keys devkey/secret) and the functions with a small Node wrapper on 127.0.0.1:8792, then open the game with `&mpapi=http://127.0.0.1:8792/api`.
`python3 -m http.server 8080`, then (real P2P/voice tests: run a local PeerJS server on 127.0.0.1:9000 path /hadal and add `&peer=127.0.0.1:9000`) `?autostart=<levelId>&mode=expedition|race&age=12&lang=de` (`?nointro=1` skips the title screen). `window.HADAL` exposes `game`, `profile`, `net`, `input` for debugging.

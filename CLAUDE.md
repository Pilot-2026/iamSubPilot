# iamSubPilot – notes for working on this repo

(Formerly called HADAL. Internal names such as the `hadal.*` localStorage keys and the PeerJS prefix `hadal-v1-` stay as they are, so saved progress and rooms keep working.)

Browser game (three.js, plain ES modules, no build step), hosted on GitHub Pages from `main`.
Owner: Andreas Schmölzer (pilotFilm). Must stay completely separate from his other games (own repo, own Firebase project). Touch controls: joystick + up/down on the right, action buttons on the left.

## Structure
- `index.html`, `style.css` – shell, HUD markup, styles (mobile + desktop); menus and dialogs are yellow glass (`--sun-glass`), wordmark `.wm` ("iam" #ffff00 with black outline, "SubPilot" #000088, roundel `assets/roundel.svg`)
- `privacy.html` – privacy policy (DE + EN); imprint is https://www.pilotfilm.at/impressum
- `assets/` – roundel, logos and portrait for the footer bar (identical to iamPilot's), `fonts/` – Source Sans 3 (self-hosted, no Google Fonts)
- Team data is state, not just events: player fields `md` (finished missions, `missionKey`), `ph` (own photos), `col` (own finds `[item id, kind]`), `atl`; all scoped to one dive by `dv` (= the dive's seed, reset at every dive start) – teammates and latecomers take everything over, nothing from an earlier dive in the same room counts
- `js/main.js` – menu (footer bar = `footerHtml()`, same links/logos/social buttons as iamPilot), profile, `Dive` class (game loop per dive, `collide()` + `drawRadar()`), results, quiz, multiplayer lobby
- `js/collide.js` – capsule shapes worked out from the 3D models, segment–segment tests (subs bounce off each other, animals/objects/ship hull are solid)
- `js/world.js` – trench terrain (heightfield), light/fog by depth, sonar mapping shader, marine snow
- `js/sub.js` – eight original sub designs (`SUB_MODELS`: isabella (mermaid sub, no. 1, built from `js/merfolk.js`, laughing face, no claw: grabs with her right hand – `info.hand` → `updateHandGrab`, the find floats into her palm), hummel, wolfshark (2nd in the picker, name plates "WOLFSHARK"), puffer, narwhal, octopus, rocket, ufo; merged parts via `js/parts.js`), movement; `RemoteSub` for teammates (model sent as player field `sub`)
- `js/launch.js` – launch from the ship (HMS Andrew): A-frame, chains, splash, underwater transition (skippable); `Recovery` = the reverse when surfacing (crane lifts the sub back on deck, then results)
- `js/schools.js` – instanced fish schools (decorative, upper ocean + twilight glow shoals)
- `js/intro.js` – title screen / menu background aquarium (the wordmark assembles itself letter by letter: one plane per letter + roundel coin, water shader, animals, subs; `?ogshot=1` = crowded posed scene used to shoot the link preview `assets/og-image.jpg`, 1200×630, keep it under 300 KB for WhatsApp; og/twitter meta tags in `index.html`)
- `js/creatures.js` – fantasy creature models, spawning, behaviour, photo/grab targeting
- `js/models.js` + `js/detail.js` – detailed real animals (incl. 10 shark species from one `buildShark()` config) and seafloor objects (lofted, vertex-painted bodies, fins, eyes); keep the userData animation hooks (fluke, tail, wings, flips, fins, bell, eyes, ears, cape, glow, strobe)
- `js/merfolk.js` – mermaid figure (`buildMerFigure` + `animMer`: segmented tail, fluke, fins, arms with strokes and waving, hair chains, blinking) in two palettes (ISABELLA sub / mermaid of Atlantis) and Neptune (`buildNeptune` + `animNeptune`: beard, crown, cape, merman tail, glowing trident). Neptune and the Atlantis mermaid are secret: only in Atlantis, never on the title screen
- `js/atlantis.js` – Atlantis, modelled on the owner's two reference pictures: pale stone + gold, facades with lit Gothic windows (canvas textures), ribbed glass domes (shader), spiral tower, glowing crystal tower, glass tubes with gold rings + pods, aqueducts, mosaic plaza with columns/statues/fountain (Neptune), glowing corals, kelp (shader sway), light shafts, whales/mantas/dolphins/fish, vehicles with headlights, a bright water backdrop. One variant per trench (`THEMES`: colours, centrepiece grandDome/crystalTower/spiral/crystals/ringCitadel/twinTowers/domeCluster, building mix, name EN/DE shown in the toast); the dive seed shuffles the layout. Static parts are merged per material (`Merger`). Every centrepiece faces the plaza and has a small glowing secret door (`makeDoor`, `portalCheck`): swimming in hides the city and shows the hidden hall (`buildHall`: starry dome, columns, treasure, mystic spotlight cones, three coloured lamps, way out through the same door; the hall grows with its creature, sub + camera are kept inside, `enter`/`exit`). In the hall lives the trench's fable creature (`js/fables.js`: Moon-Pearl Dragon/Bakunawa, Leviathan, Captain Eight-Arms/colossal octopus, Ice Kraken, Umibōzu, Flying Dutchman ghost ship, Zaratan island turtle – 4 × the sub's length, legend text + sources, `FABLE_SPEECH` = the owner's positive-thinking sentences in EN/DE, shown as yellow speech bubbles with the player's name; the creature must never be visible from outside). Appears when every mission of a dive is done (cheat: 5 × key 1, or on screen exactly 5 taps on the mission list then 5 on the radar; in Atlantis the same backwards – 5 × radar then 5 × missions, or 5 × key 1 – jumps to the fable creature); the radar is shown in every dive (deepest point ★, Atlantis A); the results show a picture of Atlantis (`snapAtlantis`) and the beings met there (Neptune, mermaid, fable creature as turntables); energy/hull/oxygen stay full there; `world.magic` clears the water near it (`world.magicCol` = theme water); buildings are solid (`cols`, `clampSub`). Team dives share the spot (player field `atl`)
- `js/guide.js` – light beacon over the deepest point (the flat deepest basin counts as reached) and the map overlay (key K / 🗺: soft fog over unexplored parts, route, finds, teammates, deepest point, Atlantis)
- `js/viewer.js` – separate small WebGL view: big rotating 3D discovery card (drag to turn), sub picker hover spin, `turntable()` sprite strips for the rotating photo tiles on the results screen
- `js/stats.js` + `js/statsview.js` + `js/data/tzcountry.js` – anonymous visitor statistics (Firestore counters `stats/all`, `stats/d_YYYY-MM-DD`, collection `visits`; local copy as fallback; owner device marked on opening, counted separately). Opened with 5 × key 9 or 5 taps on the version number
- `js/scientist.js` + `js/data/knowledge.js` – Dr. Echo (local knowledge base, optional `AI_ENDPOINT`)
- `js/data/*.js` – levels, creatures (facts EN/DE + sources), quiz (age tiers kid/junior/adult)
- `js/net.js` – rooms: **LiveKit** (default, like iamPilot but its own LiveKit project + own Netlify site: functions `netlify/functions/{join,rooms,meta}.mjs`, keys only in Netlify env vars `LIVEKIT_URL/KEY/SECRET`, setup in ONLINE-SETUP.md; `MP_SITE` in config.js, `?mpapi=` for tests, `?p2p=1` forces the fallback; host keeps meta + clock, newcomers get `sync`, positions lossy, the rest reliable; drop-outs are tolerated: a participant who disconnects is kept 45 s (lobby host 15 s), our own lost connection is re-opened with the same token (`lkRejoin`, ~90 s), everybody re-sends the whole player record every 4 s (`lkKeepFresh`), while the host is away the longest-present diver answers newcomers (`lkResponder`), the host leaving mid-dive doesn't end the dive for the others; radio publishes one gated audio track), else Firebase RTDB if configured, else peer-to-peer (PeerJS, host relays, all rooms public: codes from `PUBLIC_CODES`). `listRooms()` sends one small probe per code through the signalling server (`CANDIDATE` with `payload.probe`); hosts answer the same way with their room summary, empty codes come back as `peer-unavailable` (~5 s); taken codes without an answer get a data-connection fallback and are otherwise listed as `unreachable`. `checkConnection()` = server/STUN/TURN check shown in the multiplayer window. ICE = Google STUN + PeerJS TURN (UDP + TCP). Lonely host lobbies merge (smaller code wins); screen wake lock while in a room. `window.__FAKE_PEER__` hook for tests; `?local=1` = BroadcastChannel between tabs; Firestore leaderboard or local
- `js/config.js` – `FIREBASE_CONFIG`, `AI_ENDPOINT` (never put secrets here – public repo)
- `js/radio.js` – push-to-talk voice (PeerJS media calls between the room's peers, own channel – unrelated to the glider game's LiveKit radio) + quick radio calls; voice only for age 14+
- `js/audio.js` – synthesised FX + generative music: `TRACKS` (cheerful major/mixolydian/lydian pieces) rotate, each start/scene begins with the next one (`hadal.track`); whale song, dolphins, echo sounder always on top; sound modes all/fx/music/off (HUD button `#btnSound`, key M)
- `js/vendor/` – bundled three.js, Firebase and PeerJS (built with esbuild from npm)

## Rules
- Every real fact needs a source (`source` on creatures). Fantasy creatures must stay clearly marked (`kind: 'fantasy'`).
- All UI text in both languages (`js/i18n.js`, `{en, de}` objects in data files).
- Units: 1 world unit = 10 m. Depth in metres = `-y * 10`.
- Keep creature ids deterministic (seeded RNG) – multiplayer relies on identical spawns.
- Never copy protected designs or characters (e.g. the Beatles' Yellow Submarine): the sub is an original bumblebee design.

## Testing
LiveKit end to end: run a local `livekit-server --dev --bind 127.0.0.1 --node-ip 127.0.0.1` (keys devkey/secret) and the functions with a small Node wrapper on 127.0.0.1:8792, then open the game with `&mpapi=http://127.0.0.1:8792/api`.
`python3 -m http.server 8080`, then (real P2P/voice tests: run a local PeerJS server on 127.0.0.1:9000 path /hadal and add `&peer=127.0.0.1:9000`) `?autostart=<levelId>&mode=expedition|race&age=12&lang=de` (`?nointro=1` skips the title screen). `window.HADAL` exposes `game`, `profile`, `net`, `input` for debugging.
